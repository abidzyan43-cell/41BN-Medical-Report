Gunshot Wound Management

Emergency: Activate emergency/trauma response and arrange immediate evacuation. This is first-aid education, not a substitute for trauma-team care. Do not probe wounds or remove embedded bullets.

Illustrated first-aid poster

Non-graphic educational illustration reference; no blood or exposed wounds are shown in this module.

Choose injury location

First actions for any suspected gunshot wound

Ensure scene safety; call emergency services; use gloves/PPE; assess responsiveness and normal breathing; control life-threatening external bleeding with firm direct pressure. For life-threatening limb bleeding, use a commercial tourniquet if trained and available. Keep the person warm, monitor continuously, and expedite transport. Do not give food or drink.

Visual guide

Severe limb bleeding

Place a commercial tourniquet high and tight on the limb, not over a joint; tighten until bleeding stops and note the time. Do not loosen it intermittently.

Chest injury

Do not seal a penetrating chest wound with an improvised airtight object. Follow local trauma protocol; use an appropriate vented chest seal only if trained and available. Rapid evacuation.
